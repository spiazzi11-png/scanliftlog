Push Fitness Club

Save ScanLiftLog to your phone’s home screen for faster access at the gym. No App Store or Google Play download is required.
iPhone / iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari
  2. Tap Share
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Make sure Open as Web App is on
  5. Tap Add
Android
  1. Use your browser’s Add to Home Screen or Install option
  2. Confirm the prompt
  3. Your shortcut will open the Push Fitness gym home screen
